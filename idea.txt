You are building a small playable proof-of-concept for a browser game.

## Goal

Create a simple 3D prototype inspired by the old game Achtung, die Kurve, but with a fresh visual identity:

- Each player controls a character riding or gliding on a glowing orb
- The orb leaves a trail of the same color behind it
- Hitting a wall or any trail eliminates the player
- Trails should occasionally have small gaps, so players can pass through if timed well
- The game should feel fun alone, and even better with others

This is a POC / mock-up, so prioritize:
1. core gameplay feel
2. readability
3. simple but appealing 3D presentation
4. clean structure

Do NOT overbuild. Keep it lightweight and easy to run locally.

---

## Product vision

The vibe is:
- whimsical
- colorful
- arcade-like
- readable
- playful, not gritty
- simple 3D graphics, not realistic

The prototype should communicate the fantasy:
“Cute or expressive riders gliding on glowing orbs, leaving neon trails in a small arena.”

For this first version, we do NOT need polished art assets.
Use simple mock-up quality visuals:
- primitive geometry
- low-poly placeholder riders
- orbs with emissive glow
- glowing colored trails
- simple arena floor and walls

If character models are too much, use simple stylized placeholders:
- a sphere orb
- a tiny animal-like or capsule-like rider on top
- enough to communicate “character riding orb”

## Prototype scope

Build a small arena-based 3D game with:

### 1. Arena
- Flat enclosed arena
- Simple boundaries/walls
- Camera angle should make trails easy to read
- Arena can be circular, square, or rounded rectangle
- Use a clean, glowing arcade aesthetic

### 2. Players
Implement at least:
- 1 human player
- 2–5 AI players

Optional bonus:
- local multiplayer on one keyboard for 2 players

Each player should have:
- a distinct color
- a glowing orb
- a simple rider placeholder
- continuous forward movement
- left/right turning

### 3. Movement
Core movement should mimic Kurve-like gameplay:
- constant forward motion
- left/right steering only
- no braking needed for the first version
- smooth handling
- readable turning radius

The orb should feel like it glides, not like a heavy rolling physics object.

### 4. Trail system
This is the most important mechanic.

Each moving player leaves a solid trail behind them:
- trail color matches the orb/player color
- trail remains in the arena
- collision with any trail causes elimination
- collision with wall causes elimination

Add periodic trail gaps:
- every so often, the trail temporarily stops being drawn for a short distance
- this should create the classic “slip through” opportunity
- gaps should be visible/readable but not too large

Implementation can be simple:
- sample positions over time
- store trail segments
- use line meshes, tubes, ribbons, or another simple approach
- collision can be approximate if performant and reliable

### 5. Elimination and round flow
- When a player hits a wall or trail, they are eliminated
- Round continues until one player remains
- Show a simple winner message
- Allow quick restart with a key press or button

### 6. AI
AI only needs to be “good enough” for a prototype:
- move continuously
- turn when close to wall or danger
- survive long enough to create fun trail interactions
- not perfect, just believable

Simple heuristic AI is fine.

## Acceptance criteria

The prototype is successful if:

- It runs in browser
- A human can steer a rider/orb in a 3D arena
- AI opponents move and leave trails
- Trails match orb colors
- Trail gaps appear periodically
- Collision with trails and walls works
- Players are eliminated on collision
- A round ends with a winner
- The game feels recognizably inspired by Achtung, die Kurve
- The presentation clearly communicates “characters riding glowing orbs”
